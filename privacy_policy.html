Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Larga Na Saan Ka ("we," "our," or "the App") collects, uses, processes, and protects your personal data when you use our mobile application and related web services. We are committed to maintaining the confidentiality, integrity, and security of your personal information in compliance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and Google Play Developer Program Policies.

01

Information We Collect

We collect only the data necessary to provide regional public utility vehicle (PUV) route planning, fare estimation, augmented reality (AR) navigation, and commuter account services in San Jose del Monte, Bulacan.

account_circle A. Personal Account Information

  • Registration & Profile Details: Full name, display name/nickname, email address, password, and profile avatar (optional).
  • Demographic & Commuter Classification: Birth date, computed age, residency classification (Local Resident vs. Tourist/Visitor), and passenger privilege category (Regular, Student, Senior Citizen, Person with Disability) used strictly to compute legally mandated fare discounts under Philippine transport regulations.

location_on B. Location Data

  • Precise & Approximate Location: We access your device’s GPS coordinates via ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION to determine your origin point, pinpoint nearby loading areas and tricycle terminals, compute walking legs, and track live in-transit progress.
  • Foreground Service Location: When you actively start a navigation trip, our background-capable foreground service (TransitNavigationService with FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION) tracks your GPS progress even when the app is minimized or the screen is turned off. This is strictly used to deliver proximity arrival notifications (e.g., "Para po!" drop-off notices). A persistent, non-dismissible system notification is always displayed while this service is running, and you can terminate it at any time.

photo_camera C. Camera and Sensor Data

  • Camera Access: Requested via android.permission.CAMERA exclusively for the interactive Geo-AR Guidance feature to project real-time directional pins and terminal overlays onto your camera preview.
  • Zero Cloud Storage for Camera Feeds: Video frames and camera feeds are processed in real-time on-device memory and are never recorded, streamed, saved, or uploaded to our servers.

image D. Photos and Media Storage

  • Uploaded Images: When you voluntarily update your profile photo or attach photo proof to a transit incident report/feedback submission (activity_feedback.xml), the selected photo is uploaded to our secure database server. We do not scan or read your broader device photo library.

terminal E. Technical & Usage Data

  • App Logs & Device Information: IP address, network connection status, device model, operating system version, and system error crash logs used solely for diagnosing bugs and maintaining database connectivity.
02

How We Use Your Information

We process collected information solely for the following legitimate operational purposes:

alt_route

Transit Navigation & Guidance

Matching commuter origins and destinations to registered PUV routes, computing multi-leg transfers, and generating walking polylines.

payments

Fare Calculation

Computing base rates and distance-based fare increments tailored to your discount classification (Student, Senior, PWD).

notifications_active

Deboarding Alerts

Triggering real-time proximity alerts as you approach designated PUV drop-off points.

report_problem

Operator & Incident Management

Allowing commuters to report vehicle conditions, route deviations, or service issues directly to transport cooperatives and administrators.

shield

Account Administration

Managing your commuter profile, processing password resets, and preventing fraudulent or abusive activity.

gavel
No Sale of Personal Data: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data or location history with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

04

Data Security & Retention

Encryption in Transit: All communications between the mobile application and our database servers are secured using cryptographic protocols (HTTPS / SSL / TLS).

Storage Security: Passwords are salted and hashed before database storage. Uploaded feedback images and profile data are stored in access-restricted directories.

Retention Schedule

person
Account Data: Retained for the lifetime of your commuter account.
timer
Transit Navigation Logs: Ephemeral GPS coordinates used during live route guidance are deleted from volatile memory once the route session is finished.
history_toggle_off
Feedback Reports: Retained for up to twelve (12) months for administrative review and transport cooperative auditing, after which they are purged.
05

Account Deletion & User Data Rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 and Google Play's User Data Policy, you have the right to access, rectify, and permanently delete your personal information.

smartphone

In-App Account Deletion

  1. Open the Settings tab.
  2. Scroll to the Security section.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm your credentials.
public

Web-Based Deletion Request

If you do not have the app installed, you can submit an account and data deletion request via our public web portal:

Go to Deletion Portal arrow_forward
https://tippeenn.github.io/larganasaanka/user_delete_account.html

What Happens Upon Account Deletion:

  • Your name, email address, password hash, demographic classification, and profile picture are immediately and permanently removed from our active database.
  • Any unresolved user feedback tickets submitted under your account are anonymized.
  • Deletion is irreversible.
06

Children's Privacy

Larga Na Saan Ka is designed for general commuter use and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verified parental consent. If we discover that personal data from a child under 13 has been collected without authorization, we will delete that information immediately.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in transport regulations, system features, or Google Play policy updates. We will notify users of material changes via an in-app notice or dashboard announcement before the changes take effect.
